# Privacy

This is a personal website operated by Siddharth Sambharia. Most pages are static documents. The site does not require an account, does not ask visitors to submit personal information, and does not sell personal information.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic, popular pages, and broad discovery patterns. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's policies. The private analytics dashboard is protected separately and is not a public visitor feature. The contact email address is provided so that people can choose to contact me; any information sent there is used to reply to the message and is not added to a marketing list by this site.

The site is hosted on Netlify. Requests may be processed by Netlify and by service providers needed to deliver pages, security, analytics, or media. Server logs can contain ordinary technical information such as an IP address, browser, requested URL, and timestamp. I keep this information only as long as it is useful for operating and protecting the site, subject to provider retention policies.

External links, including links to X, GitHub, LinkedIn, Portkey, Google, and other websites, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies. If you have a privacy question or want to ask about information associated with a message you sent, [contact me by email](mailto:sam.siddharth10@gmail.com).
